Form in this verse
μεταμορφοῦσθε: verb, present, passive, imperative, second person plural
Strong’s number
G3339
Gloss
to transform, change in form
Semantic range
to change into another form; to be transfigured; to be changed inwardly
Occurrences
4 in the New Testament
Meaning in this verse

Here the word describes an inner change in believers, set against being “conformed” to this world. Context determines the meaning: the outward “transfiguration” sense in the Gospels is not imported into Romans 12:2.
