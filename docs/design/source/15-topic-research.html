Renewal of the mind

How the theme develops across Scripture

Law and Prophets
A new heart promised
Ezekiel 36:26KJV
A new heart also will I give you, and a new spirit will I put within you: and I will take away the stony heart out of your flesh, and I will give you an heart of flesh.
Jeremiah 31:33KJV
…I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts; and will be their God, and they shall be my people.
Psalms
A prayer for renewal
Psalm 51:10KJV
Create in me a clean heart, O God; and renew a right spirit within me.
Paul’s letters
Renewal as ongoing transformation
Romans 12:2KJV
And be not conformed to this world: but be ye transformed by the renewing of your mind, that ye may prove what is that good, and acceptable, and perfect, will of God.
2 Corinthians 4:16KJV
For which cause we faint not; but though our outward man perish, yet the inward man is renewed day by day.
Ephesians 4:23KJV
And be renewed in the spirit of your mind;
My summary
The Old Testament promises a new heart; the New Testament describes that renewal working out in how believers think and live.

The promise of a “new heart” in Ezekiel and Jeremiah is often connected with the new covenant described in Hebrews 8. Interpretation

Psalm 51:10 and Ephesians 4:23 both use the language of renewal for the inner life. Textual observation
