Romans 12:1

What is “reasonable service”?

The Greek phrase logikēn latreian is translated in different ways.

View
A reasonable response

Worship that makes sense in light of God’s mercy: the logical answer to chapters 1–11.

Reflected in: KJV “reasonable service”
Romans 11:36Romans 12:1
View
Spiritual, inward worship

Worship of the whole person, in contrast with temple ritual and animal sacrifice.

Reflected in: WEB “spiritual service”, BSB “spiritual service of worship”
John 4:241 Peter 2:5
Where views agree

Both readings see the whole of life offered to God as the fitting response to his mercy. The difference is emphasis, not substance.
