1. A living sacrifice 9 of 10 min

Paul has spent eleven chapters showing us the mercy of God. Now he asks for a response, and the response is not a ritual. It is a life.

Romans 12:1KJV
I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice…

In the Old Testament a sacrifice died on the altar. Paul asks for something harder: an offering that keeps living.

Pause. Ask: what did you bring to God this week?
