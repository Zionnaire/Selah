The church in Rome

The believers in Rome were a mix of Jewish and Gentile Christians, and much of the letter addresses how they belong together in one body (Romans 15:7).

Timeline
About AD 49
Claudius expels Jews from Rome (Acts 18:2). Date debated
About AD 54
Claudius dies; Jews are able to return to Rome. Inferred
Mid-50s
Paul writes Romans before travelling to Jerusalem (Romans 15:25).
