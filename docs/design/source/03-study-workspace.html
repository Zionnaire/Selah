Romans 12:1–2

Literary context

Where these verses sit in Paul's letter

Romans 11:36KJV
For of him, and through him, and to him, are all things: to whom be glory for ever. Amen.

Chapter 11 closes in praise. Chapter 12 opens with “therefore”, so the call to present our bodies follows from everything Paul has said about God’s mercy in chapters 1 to 11.

My notes

The “mercies of God” are the reason, not the reward. Preach the order: mercy first, response second. Check how “reasonable service” reads in WEB (“spiritual service”).
