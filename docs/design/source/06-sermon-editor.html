1. A living sacrifice

Paul has spent eleven chapters showing us the mercy of God. Now he asks for a response, and the response is not a ritual. It is a life.

Scripture
Romans 12:1KJVFull text in pulpit
I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.

In the Old Testament a sacrifice died on the altar. Paul asks for something harder: an offering that keeps living. It gets up on Monday, goes to the market or the office, and is still given to God.

Pulpit cue
Pause. Ask: what did you bring to God this week, apart from Sunday?

2. Rejecting conformity

The world presses its shape onto us every day. Paul’s word is a command and a warning: do not let it mould you.
